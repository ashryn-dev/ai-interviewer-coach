Why it feels real

Honest, not flattering.

Most practice tools tell you that you did great. This one scores what you actually said, for relevance, completeness and clarity, then tells you plainly.

  • It talks and listens, out loud, like an interviewer does.
  • It asks about your resume, not a generic script.
  • It gives a score and a reason for every answer.
